# Motor City Online — Complete Patch History

**Source:** Need for Speed Encyclopedia (needforspeed.miraheze.org) — retrieved May 2026
**URL:** https://needforspeed.miraheze.org/wiki/Patch_History_(Motor_City_Online)

This document contains the complete patch history for Motor City Online, from the first post-launch update (November 11, 2001) through the server shutdown (August 29, 2003).

---

## Update 1 — November 11, 2001

### Altered Content
- Starter cars for new personas have been reduced in cost.
- The cars sold in MCO Auto Sales have been reduced in cost.
- The cars in MCO Auto Sales have been given better tires to improve performance.
- Parts in Sparky T's Parts Auctions will now have starting bids that are significantly lower than their retail prices.
- Higher performance parts have been added to the parts catalog.
- All race settings are now set during race creation, and are not modifiable in the pit.
- Auctions now have direct page links at the bottom of the auction list.
- Added a timeout when players are logged in for long periods of time with no activity.
- Reduced the timeout length in multiplayer races. This was done to keep players from refusing to finish after the winner, in an attempt to bore him into quitting.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed a bug by which incorrect race rewards would be given if a host quits the race.
- Fixed a bug in the patcher that could cause infinite restart loops.
- Fixed a bug by which players could inadvertently be locked out of accessing their inventories.
- Fixed a bug which caused replays to behave erratically.
- Fixed a bug which caused the servers to think a persona was logged in when it wasn't, thus keeping some players from logging in.

---

## Update 2 — December 19, 2001

### Altered Content
- A new dealership — "Righteous Rides" — will be added to MCO. Righteous Rides offers high end cars at a discount to players who have achieved higher levels.
- Many new parts and engine blocks will be released, raising the performance bar across the game.
- A new class system for Time Trials and Multiplayer racing will be implemented. The class system will allow for more equal competition, and will enable newer and more casual players to compete successfully in weekly competitions. The system will also increase the value of the classic and vintage cars in MCO by providing competitions only available to drivers of those cars.
- "Test Install" will be added to the catalog and auctions. This will allow new users to try before they buy, thus making the process of upgrading more rewarding by greatly reducing the risk of buying the "wrong" part.
- A new multiplayer reward structure. Previously, the rewards weren't significantly different across multiplayer tracks, and when compared to Time Trials. The new system will greatly reward players for teaming up and racing with friends, and will also reward players for racing on the more challenging tracks.
- Turf war standings will be directly linked from the race lobby.
- A new set of higher end sponsored cars has been created and added to the game.
- The Filth Filter will be pre-populated with the basic nasties. Players need only enable the filter now to avoid hearing these basic bad words in chat.
- Players under level 5 will no longer be able to participate in pink slip races.
- A ticker will be added to the persona select screen to display server status.
- Car weights have been adjusted to more closely match real world specifications.
- A new option to allow / disallow NOS will be added to multiplayer race lobbies.

## Update 3 — January 23, 2002

### Altered Content
- Approximately 500 different parts will be added to both Sparky T's Parts Auction House and Parts Catalog. There are new parts for nearly every part type and every engine block family. Big Engine Blocks will have the most parts.
- New Time Trials settings for Sponsored Trials and Unrestricted Open Trials. Class Limited Open Trials will remain unaffected.

### Bug Fixes
- All 1964 Mustang should appear as 1965 Mustang to reflect proper naming for this car. No Mustangs were created in 1964.

## Update 4 — February 20, 2002

### What's New
- **Parts as Prizes** — In addition to the usual cash and points awards for the number one racer in a multiplayer race, the system will also distribute a random part!
- **Club Registration** — Clubs can now "officially register" with the Motor City Chamber of Commerce. Registered clubs can choose a club logo and a club acronym. Logos can be seen on the cars, and acronyms can be seen in chat rooms.
- **Hazard Hollow** — A brand new 4.1 mile street track designed for level 15+ racers.
- **More Parts** — Electronic Fuel Injection (EFI) Carburetors and a whole new block family: Oldsmobile big blocks!
- **More Cars** — 6 new cars, including the '69 Oldsmobile 4-4-2 and '69 Pontiac GTO.
- **Troubleshooting Tools** — New login interface tools: Port Checker, Web/Proxy Configuration, Complete File Integrity Check, Generate Error Log.

### Changes/Improvements
- Multiplayer Payouts modified — racers now earn more from multiplayer races than time trials.
- Hide Info ability removed from Persona Profile.
- Club Captain Quit/Resignation notifications emailed to all club members.
- Turf Wars Notification emails sent to all club members when a club wins a Turf.
- Turf Wars Rewards: $30,000 cash reward for winning a Turf, awarded to Club Captain.
- Drag Races improvements for consistent rewards and winning conditions.
- Improved Race Results for Time Trials — Race Time, Top Speed, First Lap Time, and Best Lap Time displayed.
- Trade Request — player name shown in chat window with trade request notification.
- U-Sell Auto Barn — thumbnail image displayed when selecting a car for auction.
- Closed Car Auctions — links disabled when auction closes.
- '57 Corvette Camshaft renamed from "57 Corvette" to "57 Corvette Camshaft".
- Spam Control — improved to reduce repeated messages in chat rooms.
- Persona Delete Interface — confirmation popup added to avoid accidental deletes.
- Improved Incident Report — allows searching for personas (online/offline).

### Bug Fixes
- Extreme tire wear under certain driving styles fixed.
- Replay camera bumpiness fixed (breaks existing replay files).
- Reset points providing unfair advantage fixed.
- Graphics display errors for Voodoo and ATI based video cards fixed (disables alt+tab and windowed mode).
- Mouse cursor loss after screenshots during replay fixed.
- Typo in Class System Info fixed: Class D should now be >= 0.07 and < 0.11 (instead of 0.7).
- Permanent Muting working correctly now — retained between game sessions.
- '57 Corvette and '58 Impala both Classic cars now.
- Incorrect engine redline calculation bug fixed.
- Calculation bug with 850 carburetors fixed.
- Pink slip races display appropriate cash winning if winner at max inventory cap.
- Drag Race results display proper winner now.
- Drag Racing display shows Total Time instead of Estimated Time.
- Lag-induced artificial red-lights in Drag Racing fixed.
- Club Races can have 2-4 players now, instead of always 4.
- Club Creation fee in Help section corrected to $5,000 (was $10,000).
- Level restrictions and prices of '70 Mustang and '70 Mach 1 in Righteous Rides fixed.
- Server selection screen displays correct ping time for correct servers.
- Community Center blank page bug fixed.
- Top Dog Standings persona search displays proper points.
- "My Top Dog" page displays correct points for players not in Top 5.

## Update 5 — March 26, 2002

### What's New
- **Bug Report Form** — In-game bug report form available from Request Help link.
- **Wind** — Wind now randomized in races with direction and velocity displayed during racing.
- **Club Un-Invite** — Club Captains and Officers can un-invite pending players.
- **Club Logo** — Previous week's Turf Wars winner's club logo displayed on Lobby screen.

### Changes/Improvements
- Weekly Paychecks increased to $1000 per level (from $250).
- Bank Balance no longer revealed when viewing another player's profile (was showing $0).
- Top Ace Inventory limit increased to 1200 (from 1000).
- Permanent Muting now disallows muted players from trading and emailing.
- Race Create/Join Timeout — 90 second timeout for blocked ports (9000-9500) or severe latency.
- Race Start — when host leaves, all readied players become un-readied.
- Race Rewards Tab UI improved during race creation.
- Parts as Prizes re-enabled with exploit eliminated.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed another tire wear issue.
- Fixed Pentium 4 front end frame rate problem.
- Fixed disappearing mouse cursor bug in Workshop.
- Fixed security violation caused by JE Ford BB pistons (1.25).
- Fixed lap time display in drag racing test drive.
- Fixed default sorting for year in auctions.
- Fixed conflict between adding contacts and muting.
- Fixed -52 error when creating club without description.
- Fixed various random crash bugs (lockups / Exception Access Violation Errors).
- Fixed saving options with fresh installs.
- Fixed server list bug causing infinite hang/retry.
- Fixed congratulation message for Top Ace rank achievement.

## Update 6 — May 2, 2002

### What's New
- **Team Trials** — New cooperative race mode for Circuit and Street Racing.
- **Hub View** — Alternate chat channel view displaying active cars of players.
- **Goose Pointe** — New 5.4 mile circuit track for level 11+ racers.
- **Classified Ads** — New Community Center feature for posting messages (For Sale, Wanted, Club Activities, Events).
- **Player Index** — Centralized listing of all players in Motor City.
- **Local News** — Replaces Competition, Commerce, and Club News; features player-generated content.
- **More Parts** — Tires, camshafts, cylinder heads, spoilers, rear ends, shocks, new engine block family.
- **More Cars** — 4 new cars including '70 Plymouth Hemi Cuda.
- **Club Title** — Customized titles for club members.
- **Club Re-registration** — Ability to change logo, acronym, and club details.
- **Persona Profile** — Text description added to "My Profile" screen.
- **New Vehicle Power Classes** — SA, SB, SC, SD (power-to-weight ratio >= 0.20).
- **End Race Camera** — Shifts to next player who hasn't crossed finish line.
- **Populated Races** — Displays list of most active racing lobbies.

### Changes/Improvements
- Improved Progression — Race rewards tuned, tracks and Righteous Rides unlocked by level instead of rank.
- Trade Interface — Warning dialog if car content changed while viewing details.
- Club Races now carry Open Race rewards.
- Class Restricted Turf Wars — each track requires different car class.
- Expanded Turf Wars Rewards — Top 5 clubs receive rewards (Captain, Top 5 Racers, all Members).
- Expanded Open Trials & Sponsored Trials Rewards — Top 10 receive rewards (was top 3).
- Expanded Top Dog Rewards — Top 5 receive rewards (was #1 only).
- Car List Sorting — Cars sorted by year in all pull-down menus.
- Garage — Now in pop-up window combined with active car selection.
- Player Hosted Auctions — Limited to 3 days maximum with new prices.
- Parts as Prizes — Random chance instead of guaranteed.
- New Player Info renamed to General Chat.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed redline bug when both gas and brake engaged.
- Fixed redline bug when downshifting using computer shifting.
- Fixed shifting problem when car hits air.
- Fixed bug where parts completely worn out after race.
- Fixed Auction paging bug (same page after clicking page 1, then page 2).
- Fixed ghost car states improving "Lag" effects.
- Fixed various vehicle auction mail notifications.
- Fixed 4 hour time discrepancy when putting cars up for auction.

## Notable Technical Details from Patch Notes

- **Server ports:** 9000-9500 (mentioned in Update 5 timeout feature)
- **Power class thresholds:** Class D >= 0.07 and < 0.11 (corrected in Update 4)
- **New power classes:** SA, SB, SC, SD begin at power-to-weight ratio of 0.20
- **Inventory limits:** Top Ace increased to 1200 (from 1000)
- **Club fees:** Registration $500,000; Re-registration $50,000
- **Turf Wars reward:** $30,000 to Club Captain
- **Weekly paychecks:** $1000 per level (increased from $250)
- **Auction duration:** Limited to 3 days maximum (Update 6)
- **Player level restriction:** Under level 5 cannot participate in pink slip races
