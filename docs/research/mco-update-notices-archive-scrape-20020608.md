## Update Notices 1
Accessible via the hub capture.

Recovered content:
- Starter cars for new personas have been reduced in cost.
- The cars sold in MCO Auto Sales have been reduced in cost.
- The cars in MCO Auto Sales have been given better tires to improve performance.
- Parts in Sparky T's Parts Auctions will now have starting bids that are significantly lower than their retail prices.

## Update Notices 2
Fetched nearest archived copy:

Recovered content:
- A new dealership - "Righteous Rides" - will be added to MCO. Righteous Rides offers high end cars at a discount to players who have achieved higher levels.

- A new class system for Time Trials and Multiplayer racing will be implemented. The class system will allow for more equal competition, and will enable newer and more casual players to compete successfully in weekly competitions. The system will also increase the value of the classic and vintage cars in MCO by providing competitions only available to drivers of those cars.

- We have implemented a new multiplayer reward structure. Previously, the rewards weren't significantly different across multiplayer tracks, and when compared to Time Trials. The new system will greatly reward players for teaming up and racing with friends, and will also reward players for racing on the more challenging tracks.
- Turf war standings will be directly linked from the race lobby.
- A new set of higher end sponsored cars has been created and added to the game.
- The Filth Filter will be pre-populated with the basic nasties. Players need only enable the filter now to avoid hearing these basic bad words in chat.
- Players under level 5 will no longer be able to participate in pink slip races.
- A ticker will be added to the persona select screen to display server status.
- Car weights have been adjusted to more closely match real world specifications.
- A new option to allow / disallow NOS will be added to multiplayer race lobbies.

## Update Notices 4
Recovered via Wayback Machine snapshot of the NFS Fandom (Need for Speed Wiki) Motor City Online / Patch History page, captured May 6, 2021. Cross-referenced against the original mco.ea.com update_notices_4.jsp hub.

**Date:** February 20, 2002

### What's New

- **Parts as Prizes** — In addition to the usual cash and points awards for the number one racer in a multiplayer race, the system will also distribute a random part! (See Parts as Prizes under Upcoming Features in the Development section of the website.)

- **Club Registration** — Clubs can now "officially register" with the Motor City Chamber of Commerce. Registered clubs can choose a club logo and a club acronym. Logos can be seen on the cars, and acronyms can be seen in chat rooms. (See Club Registration under Upcoming Features.)

- **Hazard Hollow** — This brand new 4.1 mile street track is designed for level 15 racers in Motor City. (Screenshots available on the Hazard Hollow page under Upcoming Features.)

- **More Parts** — Electronic Fuel Injection (EFI) Carburetors and a whole new block family: Oldsmobile big blocks!

- **More Cars** — 6 new cars, including the '69 Oldsmobile 4-4-2 and '69 Pontiac GTO.

- **Troubleshooting Tools** — The new login interface will provide easy access to Troubleshooting Tools:
  - Port Checker (to check if your system can communicate with Motor City servers)
  - Web / Proxy Configuration (allowing you to bypass your system default proxy settings to resolve login issues)
  - Complete File Integrity Check (automatically forces an integrity check of the game files to validate that you patched correctly and there are no corrupted files)
  - Generate Error Log (allowing you to turn on logging when requested by customer service representatives)

### Changes/Improvements

- **Multiplayer Payouts** — We have modified the payout value for multiplayer races. In general, racers will now earn more money from multiplayer races than from time trials.

- **Hide Info** — The ability to hide info in Persona Profile has been removed.

- **Club Captain Quit / Resignation** — Notification emails will now be sent to all club members when a club captain quits / resigns from the club. The interface for captains leaving clubs has also been updated to include a "yes/no" confirmation popup (to minimize the chance of "accidentally" quitting).

- **Turf Wars Notification** — Notification emails will now be sent to all club members when a club wins a Turf during weekly Turf Wars.

- **Turf Wars Rewards** — The cash reward for winning a Turf is now $30,000, awarded to the Club Captain.

- **Drag Races** — Several improvements to Drag Races have been made that ensure consistent rewards and winning conditions.

- **Improved Race Results for Time Trials** — Race Time, Top Speed, First Lap [Time] — additional columns added to the time trial results display.

## Upcoming Features Pages — recovered from archived sub-pages

### uf_team_trials.jsp (Team Trials feature page)
**Status timeline:**
- 5/8/02: Now Live
- 4/23/02: Now In Testing
- 3/5/02: Now In Development

**Description:**
- Motor City's first cooperative racing mode. Key objective: participants work together for rewards instead of competing against each other.
- Each racer tries to beat the standard Time Trial par time as a group. Clean racing matters because each racer's "time under par" is added together.
- When enough seconds under par are accumulated to reach the first award bracket, the whole team gets a Team Trial Bonus (cash + points) in addition to regular individual payouts.
- All racers who complete the race receive Team Trial rewards, even if they didn't beat par individually.
- Race types: Multiplayer Street Racing or Circuit Racing lobbies; open race type requiring an active car to create or join.
- Standard rules and rewards for Open Time Trials apply to individual participants on top of the team bonus system.

### uf_hub_view.jsp (Hub View feature page)
**Status timeline:**
- 5/8/02: Now Live
- 4/23/02: Now In Testing

**Description:**
- Alternate view of chat and racing lobbies, displaying up to 4 cars at once against a backdrop.
- Players join lobbies normally, click "Hub View" button to switch.
- Highest-level player's car pulls in first, then second highest, etc.
- When a higher-level player enters, the 4th player gets bumped from the view.
- Ties broken by who was in the lobby first.
- Players without an active car don't take up a view spot.
- 3 Hub Views exist: Street Racing, Circuit Racing, and General Chat.

### uf_goose_pointe.jsp (Goose Pointe Grand Prix track page)
**Status timeline:**
- 5/8/02: Now Live
- 4/23/02: Now In Testing
- 4/15/02: 4 new screenshots released
- 4/4/02: First description posted

**Description:**
- Second track addition to Motor City Online.
- 5.4 mile circuit track for level 11+ (subject to change based on testing).
- Located in Motor City's waterfront section.
- Four lanes wide, no road obstacles — grand prix style racing.
- Wide banked turns plus two tight S-turn sections in the city.
- Road narrows to two lanes crossing a long expansion bridge, then leads to a wooden plank area next to ship cargo bays.
- Then an open highway section, followed by a tunnel, then a difficult left turn into the historic section.
- Final wide-open stretch back to start/finish line.
- Runs fast; best line plus smart car balance wins. Two areas with tight S-turns are high-risk zones.

### uf_classified_ads.jsp (Classified Ads feature page)
**Status timeline:**
- 5/8/02: Now Live
- 4/23/02: Now In Testing

- Players pay based on number of letters and days (with caps on both).
- Players choose a category when purchasing. Visitors browse by category.
- Responses go through the in-game email system.

### GameSpot Review
- **Date:** November 21, 2001
- **Score:** 7.6/10 (Good)
- **Author:** Stephen Poole
- **URL (live):** <https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/motor-city-online-review/1900-2826690/>

- **Full text retrieved** via Wayback Machine archive.

**Key quotes:**
> "Motor City Online delivers on its potential, but it's most definitely not for casual online gamers or those whose patience is easily taxed."

> "Despite a nonracing interface that's unwieldy and cluttered, and the fact that Roxy's Diner and the Roadkill Cafe are nothing more than chat rooms rather than cool 3D environments, there are times when MCO just sucks you in."

> "If you're into racing and, perhaps more importantly, into trying to find bargains to squeeze more horsepower or handling out of a custom-built ride, you'll probably find it easy to look past MCO's shortcomings and immerse yourself in its wild, weird world."

**Review highlights:**
- Criticizes $40 price + no manual (docs all online);
- Starting car choices limited to three '57s (Bel-Air, Fairlane, Ranchero);
- Open races hard to find participants; computer-controlled opponents auto-added and win human players' cash;
- Sponsored time trials the easiest money-maker for beginners;
- Latency/lag a significant issue, especially dial-up vs. broadband;
- Auction interface clunky with no filtering;
- Top-level cars (Mustangs) auctioning for $267K+;
- Praises force-feedback wheel support, radio soundtrack, and authentic engine/horn sounds;
- Compares favorably to Grand Prix Legends / NASCAR 4 crowd — "EA took some creative license in determining car weights in an attempt to balance gameplay."

### Quarter to Three — E3 2001 Preview

- **Title:** "60 Second Preview of… Motor City Online at E3 2001"

- **URL:** <https://quartertothree.com/features/e3_2001/motor_city_online/motor_city_online.shtml>

Add in the equivalent of building up a character -- do you save up for a more powerful engine or splurge on fancy tires? -- and the comparison is a no-brainer. The best racing games are RPGs and Motor City Online is the first title to turn racing into an online massively multiplayer role-playing game. So far, Motor City Online feels as good as it looks."

## Fast takeaways
- This archive captures a live-service change log for Motor City Online from at least Jan-May 2002.
- Major themes: economy tuning, race reward balancing, class systems, content expansion, anti-griefing changes, club/community feature expansion, and lots of bug/performance fixes.
- Update 6 is a major content/system patch (Team Trials, Hub View, Goose Pointe track, Classified Ads, Player Index, Local News); 6.1 and 6.2 are clearly corrective follow-ups.
- Page 1 appears to be pre-launch/early-service notices (no visible timestamps recovered).
- **Update 4 (February 20, 2002) — recovered from Wayback Machine capture of NFS Fandom wiki. All 9 update notices now recovered.**
- Press reception was decidedly mixed. Core praise: unique car-culture MMO concept, strong customization, authentic feel. Core complaints: steep learning curve, clunky interface, lag issues, sparse population, niche appeal.
- E3 2000 Game Critics Awards: **Best Racing Game** (award given for the Motor City Online / Need for Speed: Motor City pitch).
- GameSpot news timeline: open beta May 11/Jul 25 2001 → gold Oct 23 → launch Oct 31 2001 → closure announced Feb 2003 → servers offline Aug 29 2003.
- EA's stated reason for closure: popularity problems after "the game was quickly dominated by skilled players" — new players couldn't compete and stopped playing.
- EA offered subscribers a free copy of The Sims Online or Ultima Online as compensation upon closure.
