## Key Discoveries

### GameSpot Producer Q&A (May 2000)
- Michael Waite revealed 50+ planned car models, 18 selectable race locations
- Public beta planned for April 2000 (actual beta: May 2001)
- Planned release "summer or fall" 2000 (actual: October 2001)
- Features "Tornado Flats" street track with special FX tornado

### Complete Patch History
- Documented updates from Update 1 (Nov 11, 2001) through Update 8.3 (early 2003)
- Major content additions: Goose Pointe, Hazard Hollow, Industrial Flats tracks
- New car classes: Sports Class, Imports, Trucks
- Social features: Team Trials, Classified Ads, Player Index, Local News

### Contemporary Reviews
- **IGN (7.9/10):** Praised concept but cited low player population, crashes, lag
- **GameSpot (7.6/10):** Detailed sponsored racing economy problems, auction clunkiness
- **CGW Issue 212:** Harsh review by George Jones — "awkward, plodding, inconsistent"
- **CNN/GamePro (Dec 2001):** Positive review praising soundtrack, force feedback, mechanical depth
- **CNN/GamePro (Jun 2001):** Preview with 60 base cars, shard system, 4-point physics engine

### Shutdown Coverage
- PC Powerplay #086 (May 2003): "Runs Outta Fuel" — test server closed March 2, main servers August 29
- Cited "lack of presence on the shelves" as reason for commercial failure
