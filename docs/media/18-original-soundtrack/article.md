# Motor City Online — Original Soundtrack

**Album:** Motor City Online: Original Music from the Game

**Publisher:** Electronic Arts  
**Release:** 2001

## Overview

Motor City Online shipped with a guitar-driven soundtrack heavily influenced by classic American rock, surf, and funk. The music was composed and performed by an in-house EA team led by guitarist **Matt Ragan**, with the soundtrack later released as a promotional CD.

The album was catalogued by VGMdb (Album ID 21286) as a "Commercial, Promo/Gift/Reward" release.

## In-Game Audio

The game's music files are stored in the `Data\Audio\Music` directory in **ASF format** (a container format used by some EA titles). The files can be played back with:

- **Media Player Classic**
- **foobar2000**

Radio stations are selectable from the main menu, with song titles displayed in the lower-left corner. An in-game "Tunes" menu allows players to enable or disable individual stations and tracks.
