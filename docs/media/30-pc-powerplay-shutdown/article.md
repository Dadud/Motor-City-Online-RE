## Article Text

**MOTOR CITY ONLINE RUNS OUTTA FUEL**

*EA drops Motor City Online*

After almost two years, Electronic Arts has pulled the plug on its online racing game, Motor City Online. The game's test centre server, which used to release information about new features, had already closed on March 2, but the main servers will come down on August 29. Player event submissions will continue to be accepted through the closure of the service. Critics believe that lack of presence on the shelves had prevented the game from maintaining costs let alone make profit.

---

## Preservation Context

This brief news item from the May 2003 issue of PC Powerplay provides one of the few contemporary print-media acknowledgments of Motor City Online's impending shutdown. Published roughly three months before the final server closure, the article offers two notable details:

1. **Test center server closed March 2, 2003** — A separate test/development server used for previewing new features shut down earlier than the main game servers, suggesting development had effectively ceased by early 2003.

2. **Commercial explanation** — The magazine notes that "lack of presence on the shelves" (poor retail visibility and sales) was believed to have prevented the game from maintaining costs, let alone turning a profit.

The article's headline — "Runs Outta Fuel" — captures the blunt reality of MCO's commercial failure. After nearly two years of operation, the game that had won Best Racing Game at E3 2000 and received a multi-million dollar development investment could not sustain enough subscribers to justify continued service.
