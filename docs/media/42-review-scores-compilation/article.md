## Critical Consensus

Despite the wide score range (from "D" to 92%), most major gaming publications rated Motor City Online in the **7.0–7.9 range** — generally favorable but not exceptional. The higher scores (88–92%) came from smaller or specialized outlets, while the harshest review came from mainstream newspaper critic Wes Phillips at the Tampa Tribune.

Common themes across reviews:
- **Praised:** Deep customization, authentic car culture, innovative MMO concept
- **Criticized:** Latency issues, sparse player population, slow progression, subscription model skepticism
