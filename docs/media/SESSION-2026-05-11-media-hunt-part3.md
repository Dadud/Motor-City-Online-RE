## Key Findings

### New Identified Personnel
- **Randy Dersham** — VP/GM at EA (1998–2002), Exec Producer/GM of EA Seattle
- **Michael Waite** — Producer (also on Championship Bass 2000)
- **"Jim Eomunos"** — Possibly early producer (PC Accelerator, Jun 2000); likely typo/OCR error

### Development Evolution
| Feature | 2000 Plan | 2001 Final |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Title | NFS: Motor City | Motor City Online |
| Focus | Single-player + MP | Online MMO only |
| Cars | 35 | 60+ |
| Tracks | 12 | 24 |
| Single-player | Sanctioned, Outlaw | None (EZ Street only) |

### Cut Features Documented
- Player-as-reporter for Motor City Gazette (PC Gamer Issue 87)
- Insurance ratings for drivers (PC Gamer Issue 79)
- Diner/cafe meeting places (PC Gamer Issue 79)
- Shelby Cobra as super-rare vehicle (PC Gamer Issue 87)
- Convertible roofs, opening hoods (PC Gamer Issue 79)

### SEC Filing Evidence
- EA.com restructuring announced October 2001 — same month as MCO launch
- MCO grouped with "discontinued offerings" in fiscal 2002
- Restructuring balance of $905,000 remained at December 31, 2002
- Earth & Beyond also affected by same restructuring
