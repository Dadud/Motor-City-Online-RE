# NFS-Planet: Bel Air Cup Weekly Event

**Source:** NFS-Planet (German Need for Speed fan site)
**Date:** January 24, 2002
**Article:** News #547
**URL:** <https://www.nfsplanet.com/en/news/547>

---

## Overview

On January 24, 2002, NFS-Planet reported on the newly opened **Motor City Online Community Section** and the launch of a weekly racing event called the **Bel Air Cup**.

## Key Details

### Fansite Kit
A dedicated fansite kit was made available for community members to create their own MCO-related websites and content.

### Community Section Opened
The NFS-Planet MCO Community Section launched, providing a centralized space for player-organized events and community resources.

### Bel Air Cup
A **10-week racing event** held every **Friday night on the Crossroads Atlantic track**.

The event was designed to bring the community together for regular competitive racing throughout the early months of 2002.

---

## Preservation Context

The Bel Air Cup represents one of the community-organized events that sustained MCO's player base between major content updates. Weekly events like this were crucial for maintaining engagement in an online-only game, particularly during the gap between Update 5 (early 2002) and Update 6 (May 2002).

The Crossroads Atlantic track, mentioned as the venue for this event, was one of the game's original launch tracks and remained a popular racing location throughout the game's lifespan.
