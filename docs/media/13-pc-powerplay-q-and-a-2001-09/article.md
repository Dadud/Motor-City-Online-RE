# PC Powerplay — Q&A: Is Motor City Online Online-Only?
**Source:** PC Powerplay Issue 064 (September 2001)
**URL:** https://archive.org/stream/PCPowerplay-064-2001-09/

---

## Reader Question

**From: Darren**

> Is Motor City Online an online game only? Will it be a crappy, arcade singleplayer game offline with the guts and fun of the game only online? I'm really looking forward to its release after the last effort, Porsche Challenge. I will be extremely disappointed to find I need a cable modem to play the bulk of the game. Do you know if singleplayer mode lets you play the whole game?

## Magazine Response

> Bad news. Motor City will be online only, with a small portion of the game playable offline.
>
> And unless EA launches local servers, then unfortunately you will need a cable modem.

---

## Context

This Q&A reveals early confusion among gamers about whether Motor City Online would have a substantial offline/single-player component. EA's response (via magazine) confirmed the online-only nature, which was a significant concern for players in 2001 with limited broadband adoption.
