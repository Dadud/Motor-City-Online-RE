# Motor City Renamed to Motor City Online

**Source:** GameSurge  
**Date:** September 2000  
**Archived:** 2026-05-11

---

## Announcement

In **September 2000**, GameSurge reported that Electronic Arts had officially renamed the project from **Motor City** to **Motor City Online (MCO)**, confirming its status as an online-only game.

> "Motor City is an online-only game, renamed Motor City Online (MCO)."
> — GameSurge News Archive, September 2000

## Car Lineup Description (Nov 2001)

A later GameSurge item from **November 2001** described the game's vehicle roster:

> "Motor City Online flashes back to feature classic American hot rods — custom, stocks, classics and muscle cars — licensed from the Big Three manufacturers of Ford, Chevrolet(tm) and Chrysler(tm), spanning from the 1930s through the early 1970s. Rule the streets with 60 licensed American classics ranging from the '32 Deuce Coup to the '73 Firebird Trans Am."

## Context

The rename announcement came roughly a year before the game's October 2001 launch. The shift from a tentative "Motor City" title to "Motor City Online" signaled EA's emphasis on the persistent online component, distinguishing it from traditional single-player racing games.

The November 2001 description confirms the licensed vehicle range (approximately 60 cars at that point in marketing) from Ford, Chevrolet, and Chrysler spanning 1930s–1970s American automotive history.
