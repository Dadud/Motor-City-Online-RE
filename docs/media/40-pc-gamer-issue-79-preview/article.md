# PC Gamer Issue 79 Preview — Motor City Online

**Source:** PC Gamer Issue 79 (estimated mid-2000)  
**Preservation Date:** 2026

---

## Overview

This early preview from PC Gamer Issue 79 captures Motor City Online during its pre-release development phase — likely from the summer of 2000, well before the game's October 2001 launch. The preview describes features and concepts that either evolved significantly or were removed entirely from the final product.

## Key Details

### Comparison
- **"It's Like: Need For Speed meets AMA Superbike"**
- The comparison to motorcycle racing is unusual given the game's eventual exclusive focus on classic automobiles

### Release Status
- **Release Date:** TBA (at time of preview)
- The game would not ship for more than a year after this preview

### Simultaneous Players
- Listed as **"TBA"** — indicating the networking infrastructure was still being designed

## Described Features

### Core Gameplay Loop
- Players cruise neighborhoods showing off vehicles
- Search for opportunities to race against other players
- Good performance leads to invitations to special events with large purses
- Money earned used to upgrade vehicles for future races

### Starting Conditions
- Players begin in a garage with a **"basic heap"** to work on
- Starting car requires repairs and modifications

### Car Customization
- **"Thousands of parts"** available
- Parts include: shocks, carburetors, fuel injection systems, engines, bumpers, paint
- Each part affects performance
- Engine modifications change the sound of the vehicle

### Technical Specifications
- **6,500 polygons per car model**
- Features listed:
  - Transparent windows
  - Visible interiors
  - Convertible roofs
  - Hoods that open
  - Full polygonal wheels

### Economic System
- **Dynamic economic system** tracks parts existence and value
- Value depends on quality and availability
- Parts traded through the in-game economy

### Social Features
- Races organized at **diners and cafes**
- Players meet before races to check out each other's vehicles
- **Insurance ratings** displayed for each driver
  - Rating indicates how many "accidents" a driver has been in
  - Helps players avoid racers who intentionally crash into others

### Warnings
The preview includes a prescient note:

> "BEWARE! Real-time online racing may be hindered by modem connections."

This concern proved accurate — latency issues were widely cited in contemporary reviews as one of the game's primary weaknesses.

## Evolution from Preview to Final Product

| Feature | Issue 79 Preview (mid-2000) | Final Game (Oct 2001) |
|---------|----------------------------|----------------------|
| Release | TBA | Oct 31, 2001 |
| Simultaneous players | TBA | Up to 100 |
| Meeting places | Diners and cafes | Track lobbies |
| Insurance ratings | Mentioned | Not present |
| Car polygons | 6,500 | Unknown (likely similar) |
| Convertible roofs, opening hoods | Mentioned | Unknown if present |
| Modem warning | Explicit | Dial-up supported but problematic |

## Preservation Context

This preview is significant because:

1. It documents the game during its active development phase, before the final feature set was locked
2. The **insurance rating system** is not documented in any other source — it may have been cut during development
3. The **diner/cafe meeting places** are not mentioned in any review or later coverage, suggesting they were replaced by the simpler track lobby system
4. The explicit warning about modem connections shows that even before launch, the development team and press were aware of the technical challenges facing online racing games
5. The 6,500 polygon count provides a rare technical specification for the era
