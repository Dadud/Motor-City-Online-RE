# Motor City Online Wins Best Racing Game at E3 2000

**Source:** Game Critics Awards — 2000 Winners  
**Date:** May 2000 (E3 2000)  
**Archived:** <https://www.gamecriticsawards.com/2000winners.html>

---

## Award

At E3 2000, *Need for Speed: Motor City* (as it was then known) won the **Best Racing Game** award from the Game Critics Awards committee — a panel of editors from major gaming publications including PC Gamer, IGN, GameSlice, Computer Games Online, and others.

## Judging Commentary

> *"What could be cooler than boulevard cruising and drive-in styling in a classic hunk of Detroit iron? Not much; out of the pack of slick racing games we thought EA's multiplayer hotrod crossed the finish line first. Great atmosphere, trick looks, and the potential for deeper online gameplay than most racers give this one the checkered flag."*
>
> **— Robert Mayer**, Editor, Computer Games Online

## Runner-Up

**Gran Turismo 2000** (Polyphony Digital / Sony for PlayStation 2)

## Context

This award was given during the game's development when it was still branded as *Need for Speed: Motor City*. The game would not see release for another 17 months (October 2001), and would undergo a name change to *Motor City Online* in September 2000 — just months after winning this award.

The recognition at E3 2000 highlights the anticipation surrounding the title during development. The "deeper online gameplay" praised by the judges would become the game's defining feature — and ultimately its limitation when the servers shut down in August 2003.

## About the Game Critics Awards

The Game Critics Awards are presented annually by a consortium of editors from leading gaming publications. The 2000 awards were chaired by Aaron John Loeb with associate chairs including Geoff Keighley (GameSlice), Rob Smith (PC Gamer), Brandon Justice (IGNDC), Tom Russo (Next Generation Magazine), and Frank O'Connor (DailyRadar.com).

Other winners at E3 2000 included:
- **Best of Show:** Black & White (PC)
- **Best Original Game:** Black & White (PC)
- **Best Game, PC:** Black & White
- **Best Game, Console:** Jet Grind Radio (Dreamcast)
- **Best Action Game:** Halo (PC)
- **Best RPG:** Neverwinter Nights (PC)
- **Best Online Multiplayer:** Neverwinter Nights (PC)
