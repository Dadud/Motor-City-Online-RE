## Launch Announcement

Electronic Arts' massively multiplayer online driving game, Motor City Online, is on its way to stores. The game is based on the golden age of American muscle cars, and it lets players build, modify, and race a variety of hot rods from the 1930s through the 1970s.

## Features

- Classic cars, stock cars, and muscle cars
- Customize cars with more than 1,000 real-life parts for better performance or looks
- Buy, sell, trade, and customize cars
- Race on a wide variety of tracks
- Earn extra money by winning pro races or betting with other drivers
- Weekly salary system
- **Arcade action mode:** pick a car, a track, and a race and jump right in

## Developer / Price

- **Developer:** EA Seattle
- **Retail Price:** ~$39.99
