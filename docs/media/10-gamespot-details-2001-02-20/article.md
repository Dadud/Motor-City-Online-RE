## Key Details

- **Developer:** EA Seattle (Need For Speed: Hot Pursuit, High Stakes)
- **Release:** September 2001 (actual: Oct 31, 2001)
- **Theme:** Golden age of American hot rods

## Cars

- **50+ classic cars** from 1930s–1970s
- Licensed from Ford, Chevrolet, and Chrysler
- Examples: '49 Mercury Coupe, '57 Chevrolet Bel Air, '64 Ford Mustang, '69 Pontiac GTO

## Customization

- **1,000+ real-life parts**
- Performance, aerodynamics, and appearance upgrades
- Engine blocks, spoilers, paint jobs, hubcaps, license plates

## Game Modes

- **Sim World:** Buy, sell, trade, customize cars, then race. Variety of tracks: city streets, country roads, drag strips, pro tracks. Earn extra money by winning pro races or betting against other drivers.
- **Arcade Action:** Simplified mode. Pick a car, track, and race, then jump right in.
