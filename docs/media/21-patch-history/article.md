# Motor City Online Patch History

**Source:** Need For Speed Encyclopedia (Miraheze), Need for Speed Wiki (Fandom)  
**Date Range:** November 2001 — August 2003  
**Last Updated:** Compiled from archive sources, 2026

---

Motor City Online received regular updates throughout its 22-month service life. The following patch notes are preserved from community wikis and historical archives. Dates are approximate where exact release dates were not recorded.

---

## Server Shutdown — August 29, 2003

> *"We at Electronic Arts and MCO Staff both past and present would like to say thank you for being a part of a great online racing game experience. Motor City Online service ends today, but it will live on forever in the hearts of the racers who loved the game.
>
> Thank you for all your enthusiasm for Motor City Online and making it such a great place to play.
>
> Regards,
> MCO Staff"*

---

## Update 8.3 (Late 2002 / Early 2003)

**New Content:**
- **Sports Cars:** 1966 Shelby Cobra, 1996 Toyota Supra, and 1997 Mitsubishi Eclipse now available for player ownership through Righteous Rides (one of each per player)
- **More Parts:** 200+ new parts including springs, sway bars, chin spoilers, rear spoilers, brakes, transmissions, air scoops, blowers, NOS, and tires
- **Part Auctions:** Modified for springs, transmissions, and tires; 20+ common parts removed from auction spawn but remain in parts catalog
- **Parts as Prizes:** New update parts also available through race prizes

---

## Update 8.2 (December 2002)

**New Content:**
- **Holiday Paint Jobs:** 3 specialty paint jobs for 1956 Ford F-100, 1955 Chevrolet Cameo, and 1953 Chevrolet 3100 Pickup (holiday giveaway)
- **Custom Paint Jobs:** 15 player-created paint jobs for 1949 Mercury Coupe, 1957 Ford Ranchero, 1969 Oldsmobile 4-4-2, and 1970 Ford Mustang
- **More Parts:** 50+ parts including engine blocks, crankshafts, connecting rods, pistons, exhaust manifolds, and intake manifolds

**Bug Fixes:**
- Dunbar Domed 1.0 Pistons (Buick BB), Dunbar Competition Crankshaft (Buick BB & SB), and Perfect Port Usurper Dual Plane Intake Manifold (Buick SB) no longer cause race violations
- Dunbar Premier Roller Valve Train and Dunbar Lightspeed Roller Valve Train (Buick BB & SB) no longer break cars

---

## Update 8.1

**Bug Fixes:**
- Fixed bug preventing 1957 Chevrolet Corvettes from entering Classic races
- Fixed race violations caused by Buick Small Block Piston — Dunbar Domed 1.0
- Removed Lindstorm Monster and Vindicator Blowers from Parts Auctions (existing blowers remain in inventory but non-functional)

---

## Update 8 (October/November 2002)

**Major Features:**
- **Industrial Flats:** New 4.6-mile street track through Motor City's industrial district; split into 3 segments — The Scrap Ya, The Foundry, and The Loading Docks
- **Public Race Records:** Accessible through Community Center; displays Circuit and Street Race results for up to 2 weeks
- **Imports:** 1996 Toyota Supra and 1997 Mitsubishi Eclipse (sponsored mode only)
- **Trucks:** 1953 Chevrolet Model 3100, 1955 Chevrolet Cameo Carrier, and 1956 Ford F-100 (released through auction houses)
- **Custom Paint Jobs:** 24 player-created paint jobs for 1949 Mercury Coupe, 1957 Ford Ranchero, 1969 Oldsmobile 4-4-2, and 1970 Ford Mustang
- **Sports Class:** New car class for specialty vehicles (enabled 1966 Shelby Cobra release)
- **More Parts:** Engine parts for Buick SB, Buick BB, Oldsmobile SB, and Hemi engine families; performance tires, springs, transmissions

**Changes:**
- Race rewards increased on Industrial Flats for all modes
- Base models of new trucks available through auto sales
- Club Races and Open Trials now share class restrictions
- Potter NL 30 Nm sway bar removed from catalog and auction spawns
- Competition records reset on 10/25/02 due to track setting changes
- Sponsored Races & Trials now include imports
- New track settings for Open Trials and Sponsored Trials
- Minor race reward adjustments
- Fixed visual corruption when viewing cars at distance

---

## Update 7.2

**New Content:**
- **Jade Antelope:** Story-driven car release — Ghoughakka's Egyptian vacation leads to acquiring Fast Freddy's 1959 Chevy Impala (now available through Auto Sales)
- **Beach Baby:** New custom paint job for 1932 Ford Coupe (event-only, not auction-spawned)

## Update 7.1

**Changes:**
- Stock versions of Update 7 cars available in Righteous Rides
- Custom Paint Job "Voiture de Course" now displays proper image file (old version no longer spawned)

## Update 7 (Mid-2002)

**Major Features:**
- **New Home Screen:** Simplified interface with customizable Favorites bar; all areas still accessible via pull-down menus
- **Custom Paint Jobs:** 24 player-created paint jobs (6 per car) for 1932 Ford Coupe, 1957 Chevrolet Nomad, 1965 Ford Mustang, and 1967 Chevrolet Chevelle
- **More Cars:** 4 new cars including 1970 Plymouth AAR Cuda
- **More Parts:** Rear ends, wheel rims, valve trains, carburetors, and more

**Changes:**
- Drag Racing Race Results screen cleaned up for improved usability
- Former Parts-as-Prizes parts now available in Catalog
- In-Game Help text updated
- New track settings for Club Turf Wars, Open Trials, and Sponsored Trials

**Bug Fixes:**
- Drag racing results now display correct winner consistently
- Drag racing times now add up correctly
- 1970 Ford Mustang paint jobs no longer display incorrect Boss logo

## Update 6.1

**Changes:**
- Race Rewards boosted to pre-Update 6 levels
- 345 Tires now correctly sized (were 335)
- Boss 429 now spawns with Boss engine (existing owners receive Boss block in inventory)
- Club Re-registration fee fixed to $50,000 (Registration remains $500,000)
- Final Bidding reverted to pre-Update 6 behavior

## Update 6 (Spring 2002)

**Major Features:**
- **Team Trials:** New cooperative race mode for Circuit and Street Racing; team-based clock racing with individual and team performance rewards
- **Hub View:** Alternate chat channel view displaying active cars of players in lobby (10-second delay between cars)
- **Goose Pointe:** New 5.4-mile circuit track for level 11+ racers
- **Classified Ads:** Community Center feature for posting For Sale, Wanted, Club Activities, and Events messages
- **Player Index:** Centralized listing of all Motor City players
- **Local News:** Replaces Competition, Commerce, and Club News; features player-generated content via Submit Story Program
- **More Parts:** Tires, camshafts, cylinder heads, spoilers, rear ends, shocks, and new engine block family
- **More Cars:** 4 new cars including 1970 Plymouth Hemi Cuda
- **Club Titles:** Captains and Officers can assign customized titles to members
- **Club Re-registration:** Registered clubs can change logo, acronym, and details for a fee
- **Persona Profile:** Players can add text descriptions viewable by others
- **New Vehicle Power Classes:** SA, SB, SC, and SD (starting at 0.20 power-to-weight ratio)
- **Populated Races:** Lists most active racing lobbies
- **Improved Progression:** Race rewards tuned for easier leveling; tracks and Righteous Rides unlocked by level instead of rank

**Changes:**
- End Race Camera shifts to next unfinished player
- Trade Interface warns if car contents changed during viewing
- Club Races now carry Open Race rewards
- Turf Wars are now Class Restricted (each track requires different car class)
- Expanded rewards: Top 5 Turf War clubs, Top 10 Open/Sponsored Trials, Top 5 Top Dogs
- Car List Sorting by year in all pull-down menus
- Garage now in popup window with active car selection
- Player Hosted Auctions limited to 3 days maximum
- Parts as Prizes changed from guaranteed to random chance
- New Player Info renamed to General Chat

**Bug Fixes:**
- Redline bug when gas and brake both engaged
- Redline bug when downshifting with computer shifting
- Shifting problem when car hits air
- Parts completely worn out after race
- Auction paging bug
- Ghost car state problems (improves lag effects)
- Vehicle auction mail notifications
- 4-hour time discrepancy in auction listings

## Update 5.1

**Features:**
- Troubleshoot menu: Disable Sound option (for diagnosing exception errors/lockups)
- Troubleshoot menu: Tool to reset all user settings

**Bug Fixes:**
- Sub-menu inaccessibility bug in Workshop and Auctions
- Exception error during racing

---

## Update 5 (Early 2002)

**Major Features:**
- **Bug Report Form:** In-game reporting via Request Help link
- **Wind:** Randomized wind in all races (direction and velocity displayed during racing)
- **Club Un-Invite:** Captains/Officers can un-invite pending players
- **Club Logo:** Previous week's Turf Wars winner logo displayed on Lobby screen
- **Paycheck Increase:** Weekly Paychecks raised to $1,000 per level (from $250)
- **Bank Balance Privacy:** No longer shows $0 when viewing others; simply hidden
- **Top Ace Inventory:** Limit increased to 1,200 (from 1,000)
- **Permanent Muting:** Now blocks trading and emailing
- **Race Timeout:** 90-second timeout if ports 9000-9500 are blocked or severe latency
- **Race Start:** Host leaving un-readies all players
- **Parts as Prizes:** Re-enabled with exploit eliminated

**Bug Fixes:**
- Tire wear issue
- Pentium 4 front-end frame rate problem
- Disappearing mouse cursor in Workshop
- Security violation from JE Ford BB pistons (1.25)
- Drag racing test drive lap time display
- Default year sorting in auctions
- Conflicts between adding contacts and muting
- Club creation without description causing -52 error
- Random crash bugs and Exception Access Violation Errors
- Options saving on fresh installs
- Server list bug causing indefinite hang/retry
- Top Ace rank achievement congratulation message

---

## Update 4 (Late 2001/Early 2002)

**Major Features:**
- **Parts as Prizes:** Random part awarded to #1 racer in multiplayer races
- **Club Registration:** Official registration with Motor City Chamber of Commerce; clubs choose logos and acronyms
- **Hazard Hollow:** New 4.1-mile street track for level 15+ racers
- **More Parts:** Electronic Fuel Injection (EFI) Carburetors and Oldsmobile big block engine family
- **More Cars:** 6 new cars including 1969 Oldsmobile 4-4-2 and 1969 Pontiac GTO
- **Troubleshooting Tools:** New login interface with Port Checker, Web/Proxy Configuration, Complete File Integrity Check, and Generate Error Log
- **Multiplayer Payouts:** Increased earnings from multiplayer races vs. time trials
- **Club Captain Quit Notifications:** Emails sent to all members when captain quits/resigns (with confirmation popup)
- **Turf Wars Notifications & Rewards:** $30,000 cash reward to Club Captain for winning a Turf
- **Drag Race Improvements:** Consistent rewards and winning conditions
- **Time Trial Results:** Race Time, Top Speed, First Lap Time, and Best Lap Time displayed
- **Trade Request:** Requesting player name shown in chat window
- **U-Sell Auto Barn:** Thumbnails displayed when selecting cars for Player Hosted Auctions

**Changes:**
- Hide Info ability removed from Persona Profile
