## EZ Street Mode

The designers tackled the problem of easing new players into an already vibrant and experienced gaming community. New players are often intimidated by veterans. In a game where players develop and improve their cars over time, the intimidation factor is especially high.

### Solution: Rookie-Only Venue

EZ Street is a safe haven for aspiring racers. Only the lowest-level players are allowed into EZ Street.

### Simplified Selection

- Reduced choices in terms of cars and tracks
- All specifically constructed to provide gradual learning of basic driving skills
- Still has the fun and excitement of multiplayer racing

### Training Progression

1. **Time Trials** — learn the basics of driving
2. **Pro Racing** — high-speed tracks to earn speed wings
3. **Street Racing** — tight turns, quick acceleration, unpredictable traffic
4. **Drag Racing** — quarter mile strips to test reaction times, quick shifting, and horsepower

### Quote from Development Staff

> "One of the inherent difficulties with any multiplayer game is that skills between players often vary greatly. To immediately throw a new player into the fray against vastly superior players can be downright frightening for any gamer. New players often immediately give up on games that do this, feeling they are so behind the curve that they will never be competitive. We wanted to ensure this wasn't the case in Motor City."
