# Developer Credits

**Sources:** MobyGames, Grokipedia, Sierra Wiki, GameSpot interviews, PC Accelerator  
**Preservation Date:** 2026

---

## Known Team Members

### Executive Producer
- **Randy Dersham**
  - VP/General Manager at Electronic Arts (January 1998 – January 2002)
  - Executive Producer/GM of EA Seattle office
  - Previously at Sierra Entertainment and Dynamix
  - Also credited on *Championship Bass* (2000) and *Deer Hunt Challenge* (1999)

### Producer
- **Michael Waite**
  - Credited as producer on *Championship Bass* (2000) alongside Randy Dersham
  - Gave interview to GameSpot (May 17, 2000) about Motor City Online's community features
  - Referenced as producer in contemporary press coverage

### Possible Early Producer
- **Jim Eomunos** (spelling uncertain)
  - Named as "Producer" in PC Accelerator preview (June 2000)
  - May be a typo or OCR error; no other references found
  - Could potentially be "Jim Cummins" or similar name misread by OCR

## Studio

- **EA Seattle** (Electronic Arts Seattle)
  - Known for racing titles including *Need for Speed: Hot Pursuit* and *Need for Speed: High Stakes*
  - Developed outdoor/sports titles including *Championship Bass* and *Deer Hunt Challenge*
  - Part of EA's West Coast studio network

## Unconfirmed Roles

The following roles have not been documented in available sources:

- Lead Programmer
- Art Director
- Lead Designer
- Audio Director
- Additional producers or designers

## Preservation Notes

The development team behind Motor City Online remains poorly documented. Unlike many contemporary titles, the game does not appear to have published full credits in manuals, box art, or online sources. The closure of EA Seattle and the passage of time have made it difficult to reconstruct the full team.

**If you have access to:**
- Original game manual or box credits
- In-game credits screen
- Post-mortem articles or interviews
- LinkedIn profiles of former EA Seattle staff

Please consider contributing this information to the preservation archive.
