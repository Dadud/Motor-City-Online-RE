## E3 Build Details

Since speaking with producer Michael Waite in early February, development progressed to a playable form on the E3 show floor.

### Racing Clubs

- Join groups of other drivers with shared tastes (e.g., Mustang-only or Corvette-only clubs)
- Form clubs strengthens community and increases chance for fierce rivalries
- Clubs can challenge each other to gain control of individual tracks
- Lobby menu displays the name of the club that rules a specific track
- Clubs can lose control of their tracks in weekly challenges from opposing clubs
- Ultimate goal: rule as many tracks as possible

### Dynamic Economy

- Based on supply and demand
- **Auction mode:** buy, sell, and swap parts and cars with other players
- If only one driver has a specific part, they can charge whatever they want
- Another driver can undercut the price
- Players who race often and save money can set up a shop selling customized cars to other players
- Alternative income: win pro races or wager against other drivers

### Gameplay Modes in E3 Build

- **Arcade mode**
- **Simulation mode**
- **Street races**
- **Drag racing** — two drivers sprint straight to the finish line
- **Stunt mode** — perform odd maneuvers like jumping over gaps in the road or through flaming hoops

### Public Beta

- Starting May 18, 2001
- 500 people given access initially
- More people added over a span of a few months

### Release

- Scheduled for September 2001 (actual release: Oct 31, 2001)
