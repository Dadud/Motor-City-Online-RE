# NFS-Planet: AIM Icons for Motor City Online

**Source:** NFS-Planet (German Need for Speed fan site)

**Article:** News #623
**URL:** <https://www.nfsplanet.com/en/news/623>

## Overview

On July 3, 2002, NFS-Planet reported the release of **AIM Icons** themed around Motor City Online, allowing players to represent the game in their AOL Instant Messenger profiles.

## Context

During the early 2000s, AIM (AOL Instant Messenger) was the dominant instant messaging platform among PC gamers. Custom AIM icons were a popular way for players to show allegiance to their favorite games and connect with other players outside of the game client.

The release of official or fan-made MCO AIM icons demonstrates the game's presence in broader gaming culture and the community's efforts to promote the title through everyday communication tools.

---

## Preservation Context

While the actual icon files are unlikely to be recoverable today, this article documents a small but culturally significant aspect of early 2000s gaming community behavior. AIM itself was discontinued in 2017, but the practice of game-branded instant messaging icons would later evolve into Discord avatars, Steam profile decorations, and other modern forms of gaming identity expression.
