# Motor City Online Beta Downloads

**Source:** Blue's News  
**Date:** September 22, 2001  
**Archived:** 2026-05-11

---

## Open Beta Announcement

On **September 22, 2001**, Blue's News reported that the Motor City Online open beta was available for download. Players could participate without the usual shipping-and-handling charges for physical beta discs, though registration was required.

- **Download size:** 428.2 MB
- **Registration required:** GameSpy ID
- **Primary host:** FilePlanet

## Download Mirrors

The beta was distributed via multiple sources:

| Mirror | URL | Notes |
|--------|-----|-------|

| Tiscali Games | `games.worldonline.cz/clanek/demo.asp?id=2085` | Czech mirror |

## Community Response

The Blue's News comment thread generated **97 replies across 5 pages**, reflecting significant interest in the beta. Discussion continued through January 2002, when Blue (Stephen Heaslip) confirmed that the beta had ended and players would need to purchase the retail release.

> "The beta is ended -- you're going to have to buy the game."  
> — Stephen "Blue" Heaslip, Jan 30, 2002

## Context

This open beta followed earlier closed beta phases. The 428 MB download size was notable for the era — PC Zone Issue 109 (December 2001) quipped: "Can you afford a 428Mb download?" Issue 110 (Christmas 2001) noted that those who "weren't about to delete it in a hurry" were still playing.
