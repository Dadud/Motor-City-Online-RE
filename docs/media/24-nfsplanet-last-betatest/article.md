# NFS-Planet: Last Betatest Week for Motor City Online

**Source:** NFS-Planet (German Need for Speed fan site)  
**Date:** October 22, 2001  
**Article:** News #414  
**URL:** <https://www.nfsplanet.com/en/news/414>

---

## Overview

On October 22, 2001 — one week before the game's official retail launch — NFS-Planet reported that Motor City Online had entered its **final beta testing week**. This article captures the last days of pre-launch development and community anticipation.

## Key Details

The article announced that the beta test was in its final week, with the game preparing for its official release. At this point, Motor City Online had completed its extended beta period that began in May 2001.

The announcement came just days before the game's October 29/31, 2001 North American retail launch.

---

## Preservation Context

This brief but significant article marks a transition point in MCO's history — the shift from closed/open beta testing to live commercial service. The beta period had lasted approximately five months, during which the development team refined the game's economy, racing mechanics, and server infrastructure.

The timing (one week before launch) reflects the confidence the development team had in the build at this stage, though post-launch updates would continue to address issues and add content throughout the game's lifespan.
