# PC Gamer Issue 87 — E3 2001 Preview

**Source:** PC Gamer Issue 87 (July 2001)  
**Section:** The Consensus (E3 coverage)  
**Author:** DM  
**Preservation Date:** 2026

---

## Overview

This preview from PC Gamer's E3 2001 coverage (Issue 87, July 2001) captures the game just months before its October 2001 launch. The preview reflects the excitement around the title during its final development phase, while also documenting features that were either scaled back or removed entirely.

## Key Details

### Release Information
- **Release Date:** Listed as "fall '01" (actual: October 31, 2001)
- **Coming Soon Status:** "Green light"
- **Release Date (Coming Soon listing):** 07/20/01 (this was likely a placeholder or initial target)
- **Publisher:** EA
- **Website:** EA.com

## Preview Content

### Opening
> "GET READY TO RACE FOR PINK slips. Motor City Online has been a long time in the making, but it's probably the most original of the massively multiplayer online games in development."

### Core Concept
- Online world where players join car clubs
- Race classic American roadsters for money
- Pool resources to improve fleets
- Race head-to-head for pink slips (car titles)

### Car Roster
- **Over 20 licensed cars** planned (the final game shipped with 60+)
- Range from common to super-rare:
  - Common: 1957 Chevrolet
  - Super-rare: Shelby Cobra
- The Shelby Cobra was planned to be "added sparingly to the game world one at a time"

### Physics and Racing
- Physics model described as "surprisingly high-fidelity, but relatively easy to learn"
- Expected learning curve: competitive after a few races
- Racing styles: oval-track racing and head-to-head street drag racing

### Community Features

**Motor City Gazette/Newsletter:**
- Motor City newsletter planned to keep players updated on world happenings
- Players could get a job as a **reporter on the gazette**
- Reporters would take screenshots as "photographs"
- Write their own reports from races

**Auctions:**
- "Huge part of the game world's commerce"
- Incorporate elements of eBay for buying, selling, and trading cars and parts

**Car Clubs:**
- Join car clubs
- Pool resources to improve fleets

### Customization
- Modifying stock cars described as "a big part of the fun for gearhead enthusiasts"

### Closing Assessment
> "Everyone likes a good car race. But when you add in the clique-ish feel of the game's persistent online car community, Motor City Online could be a place for racing fans to spend tons of time. This original interpretation of the massively multiplayer mantra is something we'll be tracking closely."

## Features That Changed or Were Removed

| Feature | E3 2001 Preview | Final Game |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| Licensed cars | "Over 20" | 60+ |
| Shelby Cobra | Planned as super-rare, added sparingly | Not documented in final car list |
| Motor City Gazette reporter job | Players could be reporters | Not present |
| Screenshots as "photographs" | Mentioned | Not present |
| Newsletter | Mentioned | Present as "Motor City Gazette" (automated) |

## Preservation Context

This preview is significant because:

1. It was written just months before launch, when the feature set was mostly locked
2. The **player-as-reporter concept** is unique to this preview and does not appear in any review or retrospective
3. The **Shelby Cobra** as a super-rare vehicle is mentioned here but not confirmed in the final game's documentation
4. The tone is cautiously optimistic — the preview acknowledges the game has been "a long time in the making" while praising its originality
5. It confirms the game's place in the E3 2001 hype cycle alongside major releases like *Medal of Honor: Allied Assault*
