# NFS-Planet: Motor City Online Update 6 Coverage

**Source:** NFS-Planet (German Need for Speed fan site)  
**Date:** May 30, 2002  
**Article:** News #698  
**URL:** <https://www.nfsplanet.com/en/news/698>

---

## Overview

On May 30, 2002, NFS-Planet reported on the then-upcoming **Update 6** for Motor City Online, detailing the major new features that would soon be added to the game. This article provides a contemporary snapshot of how the game's community and fan sites covered ongoing development.

## Key Features Previewed

### Hub View
A new alternate chat channel view that displays the active cars of players currently in the lobby. The display cycles through players' cars with a 10-second delay between each vehicle.

### Goose Pointe
A new 5.4-mile circuit track restricted to level 11+ racers.

### Team Trials
New cooperative race mode for both Circuit and Street Racing. Teams race against the clock with individual and team performance rewards.

### Classified Ads
A Community Center feature allowing players to post messages for:
- For Sale
- Wanted
- Club Activities
- Events

### Player Index
A centralized listing of all Motor City players.

### Local News
Replaced the previous Competition, Commerce, and Club News sections. Featured player-generated content through the Submit Story Program.

### New Content
- More parts: tires, camshafts, cylinder heads, spoilers, rear ends, shocks, and a new engine block family
- More cars: 4 new vehicles including the 1970 Plymouth Hemi Cuda

### Social Features
- **Club Titles:** Captains and Officers can assign customized titles to members
- **Club Re-registration:** Registered clubs can change logo, acronym, and details for a fee
- **Persona Profile:** Players can add text descriptions viewable by others

### Gameplay Changes
- New vehicle power classes: SA, SB, SC, and SD (starting at 0.20 power-to-weight ratio)
- End Race Camera shifts to next unfinished player
- Race rewards tuned for easier progression
- Tracks and Righteous Rides unlocked by level instead of rank
- Populated Races list showing most active racing lobbies
- Trade Interface warns if car contents changed during viewing
- Club Races now carry Open Race rewards
- Turf Wars are now Class Restricted (each track requires different car class)
- Expanded rewards for Top 5 Turf War clubs, Top 10 Open/Sponsored Trials, and Top 5 Top Dogs
- Car List Sorting by year in all pull-down menus
- Garage now in popup window with active car selection
- Player Hosted Auctions limited to 3 days maximum
- Parts as Prizes changed from guaranteed to random chance
- New Player Info renamed to General Chat

### Bug Fixes
- Redline bug when gas and brake both engaged
- Redline bug when downshifting with computer shifting
- Shifting problem when car hits air
- Parts completely worn out after race
- Auction paging bug
- Ghost car state problems (improves lag effects)
- Vehicle auction mail notifications
- 4-hour time discrepancy in auction listings

---

## Preservation Context

This NFS-Planet article provides a window into how the active MCO community received major updates through fan sites. The detailed feature list demonstrates the substantial ongoing development the game received more than seven months after its October 2001 launch.

Update 6 represented one of the most significant content drops in the game's history, introducing entirely new social systems (Classified Ads, Player Index, Local News) and cooperative gameplay (Team Trials) alongside traditional racing content.
